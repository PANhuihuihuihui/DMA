<objective>
Automatically mark a publish job `manual_fallback_required` when direct publishing is blocked by a terminal, non-retryable failure (STATUS-03), consistently across the Facebook and TikTok publish paths, while leaving retryable failures on the existing retry path.

Purpose: give pilot operators a clear terminal state for blocked jobs without exposing secrets, reusing the existing failure taxonomy and publish lifecycle.
Output: a shared store-level fallback policy + marker, publisher wiring on both paths, and tests.
</objective>
