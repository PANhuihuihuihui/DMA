# 03-01 Plan: Predis Replica Plus Local Proof Loop MVP

## Goal

Build a customer-demo-ready product surface that replicates Predis.ai's core loop closely enough for a buyer to recognize feature parity, then makes it 1% better for local SMBs with owner approval, platform-native local strategy, and an honest measurable response loop.

The target is not a generic scheduler. The target is:

> one local business idea -> branded posts, videos, carousels, captions, hashtags, calendar slots, approval, publishing, and response evidence.

## Research Summary

### Predis Parity Features To Match

Local research and current public Predis pages identify these parity requirements:

- AI content generation from a prompt into social posts, videos, carousels, captions, memes, hashtags, and ads.
- Brand kit / brand management with logo, colors, tone of voice, key messaging, and connected accounts.
- Creative editor for generated single-image posts, videos/reels, carousels, and stories.
- Multi-platform scheduling and calendar management.
- Account linking and direct publishing / scheduling to major channels.
- Platform-specific editing and tailored posts for each channel.
- Content approval flow, draft post management, comments, notes, and collaboration.
- Bulk generation and bulk scheduling for weekly/monthly campaigns.
- Competitor analysis with content themes, hashtag sets, posting frequency, and best-time signals.
- Analytics for post performance, best-performing days, engagement times, posting frequency, and follower growth.
- Mobile-friendly and self-serve pricing/usage framing through brands, credits, connected channels, and generation speed.

### 1% Better LocalPilot Layer

The v3 research says the opening is not "better AI captions." The opening is a complete local-business loop:

- Owner approval is the default trust boundary, not an enterprise-only afterthought.
- One idea becomes different local strategies per platform, not one caption lightly rewritten across channels.
- Every publishable post can carry a measurable response hook: short link, QR code, phone CTA, direction CTA, booking CTA, DM keyword, coupon code, or owner-confirmed mention.
- Reporting must be honest lower-bound evidence, not exact offline ROI attribution.
- The beachhead demo should feel like it was built for restaurants, cafes, salons, HVAC, clinics, and local shops, not only e-commerce stores or agencies.

## Product Principle

Clone Predis's loop, not its generic positioning.

Predis sells "generate, design, schedule." LocalPilot should demonstrate "generate, design, approve, publish, and prove something happened locally."

## Demo Scope

### Must Ship In Phase 3

1. Predis-style workspace shell:
   - dashboard
   - brand kit
   - AI generator
   - creatives/editor
   - content calendar
   - approval queue
   - connected accounts
   - competitor ideas
   - analytics/proof loop

2. Business and brand kit:
   - business name, industry, location, language, audience, offer type, tone, goals
   - logo/image placeholder
   - colors
   - voice rules and "words to use / avoid"
   - example past posts or reviews for tone calibration

3. One prompt -> weekly content batch:
   - user enters one local idea, promotion, menu item, service, product, event, or photo note
   - system creates a weekly batch with at least Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, and Google Business Profile draft outputs
   - optional LinkedIn, X, Pinterest, and YouTube Shorts appear as future/locked parity placeholders so the product map mirrors Predis without overbuilding integrations

4. Platform-native creative outputs:
   - Facebook community post
   - Instagram feed post or carousel
   - TikTok/Reels short video script
   - Google Business Profile update
   - caption, CTA, hashtags, media direction, compliance notes, and local proof hook per output

5. Creative preview/editor:
   - post card preview
   - carousel slide outline preview
   - reel/video script preview
   - editable caption, hashtags, CTA, media notes, proof hook, and schedule slot
   - version history remains backend-owned

6. Calendar and scheduling:
   - drag/drop-like weekly calendar surface, implemented initially as selectable slots if full drag/drop is too expensive
   - schedule metadata stored on backend draft records
   - calendar filters by platform and approval status

7. Owner approval flow:
   - approve exact draft version
   - request changes
   - approve all safe drafts in the weekly batch
   - approval snapshot freezes payload before publishing

8. Real publishing path:
   - keep Facebook OAuth connect and live Page publishing as the first real adapter
   - no user-pasted access tokens
   - TikTok/Instagram/Google can remain assisted or fake-provider outputs in this phase unless official access is already available

9. Competitor / Idea Lab parity:
   - local competitor URL/profile input fields
   - generated idea cards for content themes, hooks, hashtag clusters, and posting time suggestions
   - clearly mark as "demo analysis" until real ingestion is built

10. Proof Loop:
    - generate per-post short-link/QR/coupon placeholders
    - record demo events: link click, call tap, direction tap, booking click, DM keyword, coupon redemption, owner-confirmed mention
    - show weekly evidence summary by platform and post
    - label as "measurable response" or "proof loop"; do not call it exact ROI

11. Demo readiness:
    - a restaurant/cafe demo can be run end-to-end locally
    - a customer sees an idea become publishable assets and at least one real Facebook Page post if OAuth is configured

### Must Not Ship In Phase 3

- Paid ad campaign booking or budget management.
- Scraping, cookie posting, or browser automation for production publishing.
- Asking customers to paste access tokens.
- Exact offline attribution or revenue claims.
- Full agency white-label workspace.
- Native Xiaohongshu publishing.
- Full video rendering pipeline beyond script/storyboard/static preview unless an existing low-risk tool is adopted.

## Screens And UX Contracts

### Screen 1: Dashboard

Purpose: Make the app feel like an AI social media manager, not a single form.

Required blocks:

- This week's content status.
- Brand health / connected accounts.
- Pending approvals.
- Proof loop summary.
- "Create content" primary CTA.
- Recent generated batches.

### Screen 2: Brand Kit

Required blocks:

- Business profile.
- Logo/visual placeholder.
- Brand colors.
- Voice and language.
- Past post/review examples.
- Local context: city, neighborhood, service radius, popular items/services, offers, restrictions.

### Screen 3: AI Generator

Required blocks:

- Prompt box for local idea.
- Campaign objective selector.
- Platform selection.
- Format selection: post, carousel, reel/script, story, ad placeholder.
- Generate weekly batch CTA.
- Generation result summary with editable creative cards.

### Screen 4: Creative Editor

Required blocks:

- Visual card preview.
- Caption editor.
- Hashtag editor.
- CTA/proof hook editor.
- Platform-specific checklist.
- Version history.
- Save draft / request rewrite / send to calendar.

### Screen 5: Content Calendar

Required blocks:

- Weekly or monthly calendar grid.
- Platform chips.
- Status chips: draft, needs review, approved, scheduled, published, failed.
- Slot details panel.
- Schedule/reschedule controls.

### Screen 6: Approval Queue

Required blocks:

- Drafts grouped by campaign and platform.
- Exact-version approval button.
- Request changes button.
- Approve-all safe drafts.
- Frozen approval snapshot preview.

### Screen 7: Connected Accounts

Required blocks:

- Facebook connected via OAuth.
- Instagram/TikTok/Google shown as planned or assisted depending on available adapter.
- Permission health.
- Reconnect or setup guidance.
- Explicit note that credentials stay server-side.

### Screen 8: Competitor Ideas

Required blocks:

- Competitor profile/page URL input.
- Idea cards: theme, hook, format, timing, hashtags.
- Demo/fake analysis badge until real ingestion exists.

### Screen 9: Proof Loop Analytics

Required blocks:

- Post-level response events.
- Weekly evidence summary.
- QR/short-link/coupon preview.
- Owner-confirmed mention input.
- Copy that says "evidence signals" and avoids exact ROI precision.

## Acceptance Criteria

Phase 3 is done when:

1. A local restaurant/cafe profile can be configured with brand kit and local context.
2. One promotion or idea generates a weekly content batch with platform-native outputs for Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, and Google Business Profile.
3. The UI visibly covers Predis-like brand kit, generator, creative editor, calendar, approvals, connected accounts, competitor ideas, and analytics.
4. Drafts can be edited, versioned, approved, scheduled, and reviewed.
5. Facebook can be connected through OAuth and an approved Facebook draft can publish live when Meta credentials are configured.
6. Other channels produce honest assisted publishing packages when official publishing is not implemented.
7. Every publishable output can carry a proof hook and show response events in the proof-loop screen.
8. The app never asks the customer to paste access tokens.
9. The app never claims exact offline ROI.
10. The demo is credible enough for a customer walkthrough without explaining that "the real app is somewhere else."

## Risks

- Exact Predis UI may differ behind login. Mitigation: Slice 1 uses user-assisted login and screenshots before implementation finalization.
- Full video rendering is expensive. Mitigation: ship reel/script/storyboard preview first, not rendered video.
- Meta app review may block production. Mitigation: local demo uses configured dev/test app and clearly documents review requirements.
- Proof loop can sound like fake ROI. Mitigation: use evidence language and lower-bound metrics only.
