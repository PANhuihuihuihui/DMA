# 10-Restaurant Outreach Playbook — Ann Arbor pilot

> Date: 2026-06-17
> Product name placeholder: `Brickbeat` (replace if final name changes)
> Offer: $99/mo, 3-month pilot, first 10 restaurants only
> Positioning: "local marketing rhythm + measurable response signals" — not exact attribution
> Rule: Do NOT cold-spam. Use warm intro, in-person visit, or value-first email with a specific observation.

---

## Core pilot offer

**What they get**

- Weekly content plan for Facebook / Instagram / TikTok
- Owner-approved posts, not auto-spam
- One idea or photo → platform-native posts
- Short links / QR / coupon code for measurable response signals
- Weekly email: clicks, call taps, direction taps, coupon redemptions, owner-confirmed mentions

**What they pay**

- $99/mo
- 3-month pilot
- No setup fee for first 10 restaurants
- Cancel after 3 months if not useful

**What we need from them**

- 1 weekly promotion/menu/service idea OR 3-5 photos/videos
- 5 minutes/week to approve posts
- Agreement to share anonymized metrics if successful
- Staff willingness to ask: "Did you see us on Instagram/TikTok?" and mark coupon redemptions if used

**What we should NOT promise**

- Exact ROI
- Guaranteed walk-ins
- Viral TikTok growth
- Hands-off posting with no approval

**What we can promise**

- Less guessing
- More consistent posting
- Platform-native content
- Measurable response signals
- Owner control

## Outreach principle

The Reddit signal is clear: SMBs hate fake-personalized marketing spam.

So every outreach must include:

1. **A real observation** from their public story/menu/social page
2. **A small useful idea** they can use even if they ignore us
3. **A low-friction ask**: 15 minutes or a free one-week sample
4. **No fake claim** like "I noticed your website traction"
5. **No pressure** and no automation tone

## What NOT to say

Avoid:

- "I noticed your SEO..."
- "Your social media is underperforming"
- "We guarantee more customers"
- "AI will run your marketing"
- "Exact ROI attribution"
- "We can make you viral"

Use:

- "I read your story / saw your menu / liked your event"
- "Would a free sample be useful?"
- "Owner-approved"
- "Response signals, not exact attribution"
- "Less guessing"

## Tracking the pilot

Create a spreadsheet with:

| Field | Description |
|---|---|
| Restaurant | Name |
| Owner/contact | Public owner/contact or warm intro |
| Source | How we found them |
| Specific observation | One real thing from interview/menu/social |
| First message date | Date |
| Follow-up date | Date |
| Sample sent? | Yes/no |
| Pilot status | no response / sample / call / closed / rejected |
| Objection | Time / price / not interested / already has agency / doesn't trust AI |
| Learning | What to change |

Pilot success metric:

- 10 free samples sent
- 5 calls booked
- 3 paid pilots closed
- 2 retained after first month

If we cannot close 3 out of 10 with highly personalized outreach + free sample, the wedge or offer is weak.
