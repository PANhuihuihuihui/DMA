## How v3 re-anchors competitor features against pain signals

For each v3 competitor feature, we identify the pain signal it serves (or fails to serve):

### Scale Social
- ✅ Solves: "I want authentic content" (their value prop is real customers, not AI bots)
- ✅ Solves: "I have no time to create" (their flywheel does it for you)
- ❌ Doesn't solve: "I'm a single-location SMB with 50 customers/week" (their model needs volume)
- ❌ Doesn't solve: "I want strategy, not just content" (their AI Director is scorer, not strategist)

### Youzan AI 内容创作
- ✅ Solves: "I don't know what to post" (AI templates + topic library)
- ✅ Solves: "I have no time" (auto-publish + half/full-auto modes)
- ✅ Solves: "I want UGC" (碰碰贴 in-store NFC)
- ❌ Doesn't solve: Cross-platform (XHS-only)
- ❌ Doesn't solve: English / US SMBs (China-locked)

### Predis.ai
- ✅ Solves: "I don't know what to post" (one prompt → full post)
- ✅ Solves: "Brand consistency" (brand kit auto-applied)
- ❌ Doesn't solve: "Auto-post without review" (their docs say "not recommended")
- ❌ Doesn't solve: "Real attribution" (only ad-performance vanity)
- ❌ Doesn't solve: "Multi-platform native strategy" (same template, slight variants)

### Nectar Social
- ✅ Solves: "I have too many DMs/comments to reply" (AI triage)
- ✅ Solves: "Brand voice consistency in replies"
- ❌ Doesn't solve: Content creation (only handles inbound)
- ❌ Doesn't solve: SMB pricing (enterprise-only)

---

## Conclusion for v3

**No competitor solves the FULL wedge** (one input → strategy → native posts → approval → publish → attribution).

The closest competitor to the full wedge is **Youzan**, but it's China-locked and XHS-only.

In the US, **Predis.ai** is the closest feature-comparable, but it explicitly admits "fully automated posting not recommended" — i.e., they punt on the owner-approval workflow.

**Our wedge is unoccupied in the US market.**
