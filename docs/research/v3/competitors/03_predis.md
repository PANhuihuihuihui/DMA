## 1. What it is

**Predis.ai** — AI social media content creation + scheduling platform. Generates **branded posts, videos, and carousels from text prompts**. Targets solo creators, small businesses, e-commerce, and agencies.

Positioned as: "AI ad generator" + "social media scheduler" + "brand kit manager".

## 2. Pricing (verified, 2026)

| Plan | Monthly | Annually | Includes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Solo** | $32/mo | **$19/mo** | 1 brand, **30 AI posts/month**, brand kit, scheduling, captions, hashtags, basic analytics |
| **Business** | $79/mo | $40/mo | 5 brands, 100 AI posts/month, all Solo + carousel + video + competitor analysis + team collab |
| **Agency** | $249/mo | $212/mo | Unlimited brands, 400 AI posts/mo, white-label, API access |

Key: **no free plan**. The $19/mo annual Solo is the value anchor.

## 5. Who uses it today

From customer testimonials (predis.ai homepage):
- Solo creators / coaches
- E-commerce store owners
- Social media managers handling multiple clients
- Agencies (white-label tier)

**NOT primary**: local brick-and-mortar SMBs, restaurants, salons, etc. It's positioned for **digital-first** small businesses.

## 6. The pricing reality for our beachhead

A US independent restaurant wanting to do daily Instagram + TikTok + Facebook:
- Predis Solo $19/mo (annual) → 30 posts/mo = **1 post/day across ALL platforms combined**
- That's not enough for a 3-platform daily strategy
- To get 100 posts/mo they need Business $40/mo (annual)
- At 1-2 posts/day per platform they need $79/mo minimum

→ Our $99-199/mo competes head-on at the upper end. But our promise is **"one idea → 4 platform-native posts"** which is more output per dollar.

## 7. Marketing hook they use

> "Predis.ai cuts post creation time by 60 to 70 percent for static posts and carousels, and the scheduling calendar is highly rated."

→ **"60-70% time saved"** is their #1 marketing claim. We should A/B test this in our own copy.

## 8. Key takeaway for v3 report

**Predis.ai is the closest US-market competitor to our wedge** — same input model (one prompt → post), similar price point, similar target.

**What they WIN on:**
- Established market presence (since ~2022)
- Mature product with multiple plans
- Solo pricing $19/mo anchors the category low
- Strong content marketing / SEO presence

**What we WIN on (vs Predis):**
- **Owner approval as a feature** (they admit "fully automated posting not recommended")
- **Multi-platform native strategy** (they generate same-shape post for IG/TikTok; we rewrite per-platform)
- **Conversion attribution** (they have "ad performance"; we have calls/DMs/walk-ins)
- **SMB-specific** (they target e-comm + agencies; we target restaurants/salons)
- **No platform limits** (their $19 = 30 posts/mo; we offer weekly autopilot without hard cap)

**The killer differentiation**:
> Predis says "60-70% time saved on creation."
> We say "60-70% time saved, plus owner-approved, plus real attribution to calls/bookings."

The second half is our moat.
