## 1. Who they actually are (corrected from v2)

**Scale Social** = a US-based AI marketing startup, NOT a XHS-specific tool.

- **Funding**: $1.3M Pre-Seed (2026)
  - Investors: **LAUNCH** (by Jason Calacanis), **The Syndicate** (Jason Calacanis), **NC IDEA Seed Grant**
  - HQ: US (NC IDEA = North Carolina)
- **Tagline**: "AI Powered UGC Supply-Chain" / "Turn your customers into your greatest storytellers"
- **Web**: scalesocial.ai (also getsocialscale.com)

## 2. What they actually do (the workflow)

```
[Customer at venue] 
  → [Scan QR / "碰碰贴"-style prompt] 
  → [Customer records experience on phone] 
  → [AI auto-generates short video ad] 
  → [Distribute to local Instagram, Facebook, Google]
```

Target verticals: **chain restaurants, fitness studios, beauty, experience brands**.

Their reported metric: "最高 104% 销售增长" (up to 104% sales growth) for partner brands.

## 3. Full feature list (from scalesocial.ai)

| Module | What it does |
|---|---|
| **AI Director** | Trained on brand guidelines + audience data; runs full content workflow |
| **Capture / Source** | Pull from organic customer content, creator partnerships, multi-channel prompts at point of experience |
| **AI Moderation** | Auto-review for brand safety, rights, quality — no manual review needed |
| **AI Scoring** | Predicts performance BEFORE spend; ranks all approved assets |
| **Deploy** | Ready-to-run creative across channels |
| **Real-time Optimization** | Budget shifts toward outperforming content without post-campaign review |
| **Brand training** | Trained on each client's brand + audience; library compounds over time |

- **UGC + creator + organic content** (not owner-input → strategy engine)

## 7. How this updates our v2 risk assessment

❌ **v2 was WRONG**: I wrote "Scale Social (小红书) — Just raised, China market"
✅ **Reality**: Scale Social is **US enterprise UGC chain tool**. Different wedge entirely.

**What this means for LocalPilot:**
- **Less direct threat than v2 claimed** — they serve enterprise chains, we serve SMBs
- **But the UGC harvesting approach is genuinely novel** — customers-as-creators bypasses the "AI content = spam" objection
- **Opportunity**: their product requires multi-location traffic (volume to feed the AI loop). A single restaurant with 50 customers/week can't generate enough UGC for their model. That's OUR wedge.
- **Risk if they expand downmarket**: their AI Director model could be reconfigured for SMB if they raise Series A.

## 8. Key takeaway for v3 report

**Scale Social is NOT a direct competitor today.** They validate that "AI marketing for local businesses" is a fundable category, but they're building the **enterprise UGC flywheel**, not the **SMB strategy autopilot**. Our wedge remains unoccupied.

However, watch them — if they raise a Series A within 12 months and release an SMB tier, we'll be in the same market.
