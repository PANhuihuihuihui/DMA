The CEO Verdict (60-second read)

The market is real and growing. TAM = $32.48B (2025) → $164.52B (2034), CAGR 19.70%. 36M US SMBs, 54% increasing social spend.

Our wedge is unoccupied in the US SMB segment. No competitor combines: (1) one prompt → multi-platform strategy, (2) owner-approval workflow, (3) real attribution to calls/bookings/DMs.

Three things must be true for the wedge to hold: ① Ship the conversion attribution loop (our #1 moat). ② Brand against AI-spam with provable real outcomes. ③ Avoid the Youzan trap (don't try to compete in China).

4The 3 features NOBODY ELSE has

🏆 Feature 1 — Real attribution loop

Per-post QR code + short URL → owner sees clicks, calls, DMs, reservations attributed to each post in a weekly email.

"Total spent: $150. Total posts: 6. Total likes: ~3500. Total comments: ~500. Visitors in page: 0. Conversions: 0. It's literally bots. Bots everywhere."
— Reddit r/smallbusiness 1qfie00 (454↑, 45 comments) — the "Dead Internet" pain

No competitor has this for SMBs. Youzan only tracks 团购核销 (group-buy redemptions). Nectar integrates with Klaviyo (email) and Attentive (SMS) but not calls/bookings. Scale Social, Predis, Buffer, Hootsuite all stop at vanity metrics.

🏆 Feature 2 — Strategy engine (not just AI captions)

One input → four platform-native STRATEGIES. TikTok = trend-jacking hook + 30-sec vertical video. Instagram = carousel aesthetic + lifestyle. Facebook = community post + engagement prompt. Xiaohongshu = 种草 structure + keyword-rich caption.

"I absolutely SUCK at social media. What is everyone's secret to social marketing and coming up with content that people give a shit about?"

Top reply: "The secret isn't posting more, it's posting what buyers already say out loud. Stop thinking like a brand and start mining Reddit, Etsy reviews, or TikTok comments for phrases real people use..."
— Reddit r/smallbusiness 1m3af5h (56 comments) — the "what to post" pain

Predis.ai explicitly generates the same post with minor variants per platform. We rewrite the marketing logic per platform. This is real differentiation.

🏆 Feature 3 — Owner approval as first-class feature

Every post requires owner tap. Email + WhatsApp notification. 1-click approve. Cannot be bypassed.

"Dead Internet. Bots and AI talking to Bots and AI."

"LinkedIn has gotten really, really bad... a few operations out there ghostwriting content and then circle-jerking responses."
— Reddit r/smallbusiness 1qfie00 comments — market sentiment on AI content

Predis.ai's docs explicitly say "fully automated posting not recommended" — they punted on the approval workflow. We make it our promise. Scale Social auto-deploys. Youzan has approval but only via DingTalk/WeCom (China-locked). We're the only US SMB tool with first-class approval.
