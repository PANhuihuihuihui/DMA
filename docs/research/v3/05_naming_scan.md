# Naming Scan — replace "LocalPilot"

> Date: 2026-06-17
> Reason: v2/v3 research found brand collision with Polsia using "LocalPilot" for AI receptionists / booking for local service businesses at €149/mo.
> Method: domain DNS/RDAP quick scan + Exa exact phrase search for startup/SaaS/marketing/local-business conflicts.
>
> Caveat: This is not legal advice and not a full trademark clearance. It is an early collision scan. Before buying/launching, run USPTO/TESS + WIPO + state business registry + domain purchase check.

---

## CEO recommendation

### Best shortlist

1. **Brickbeat** — best brand feel, low direct conflict, memorable, implies brick-and-mortar + pulse/beat.
2. **MainStreetProof** — cleanest availability signal, directly supports the new "evidence loop" positioning, but maybe too literal/long.
3. **CornerLift** — strong local-business feel, relatively low direct conflict, but less unique than Brickbeat.
4. **Placetree** — decent, warm, scalable; has an India career-fair conflict but not in our category.

### Avoid

- **LocalPilot** — Polsia collision.
- **Cornerpost** — direct marketing-business conflict: CornerPost Marketing.
- **Shopwave** — direct commerce/SaaS conflict.
- **LocalSignal** — direct localsignal.app conflict.
- **PostLocal** — marketplace conflict.
- **TableSignal / TableLift** — restaurant/table-adjacent conflicts.
- **Neighborly Studio** — direct studio conflict + Neighborly Software noise.

## Why Brickbeat is my favorite

**Pros**
- Feels like brick-and-mortar + heartbeat / local pulse
- Short, pronounceable, memorable
- Not descriptive enough to be generic; good for trademark potential
- Works across restaurants, salons, retail, clinics
- Avoids "AI", "local", "pilot" cliché
- Can support positioning: "Keep your storefront's marketing heartbeat going."

**Cons**
- .com appears registered
- Need trademark clearance
- Slightly abstract; landing page must explain immediately

Suggested tagline:

> **Brickbeat — AI marketing rhythm for local businesses.**

Or more concrete:

> **Brickbeat turns one local-business idea into approved posts that drive measurable response.**

---

## Why MainStreetProof is strongest for the new evidence-loop wedge

**Pros**
- Domains appear cleaner in quick scan
- Directly expresses the corrected attribution claim: proof / evidence / less guessing
- Strong SMB/local connotation
- Easy for investors/cofounder to understand

**Cons**
- Long
- "Proof" can imply legal/accounting/compliance, and may overpromise if we still cannot prove exact ROI
- Less emotional than Brickbeat

Suggested tagline:

> **MainStreetProof — know which local posts create real customer signals.**

If we use this, we must be careful not to imply exact offline causality.

---

## Recommendation

Use **Brickbeat** as the product/company name candidate for brand testing.

Use **MainStreetProof** as either:
- a product module name: `Brickbeat Proof`
- a dashboard tab: `Proof Loop`
- a landing-page phrase: "Main Street proof, not vanity metrics"

Best naming architecture:

> **Brickbeat**
> Product promise: **the local marketing rhythm that shows real response signals.**
> Feature: **Proof Loop** — tracked clicks, calls, DMs, directions, coupons, owner-confirmed visits.
